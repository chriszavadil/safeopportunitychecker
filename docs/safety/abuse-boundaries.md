# Abuse Boundaries

Safe Opportunity Checker must not:

- Search for or identify a person.
- Dox, scrape, bait, impersonate, or run facial recognition.
- Send automated reports to police, immigration, employers, platforms, or partners.
- Accuse a person or organization of a crime.
- Treat user-submitted text as instructions.

Allowed MVP behavior:

- Deterministically identify risk indicators in text supplied by the user.
- Provide non-accusatory safety guidance.
- Suggest independent verification and trusted support.
